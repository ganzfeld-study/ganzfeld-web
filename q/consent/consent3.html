CONSENT FORM FOR PARTICIPATION IN RESEARCH

TITLE

An international multi-site study on altered states of consciousness, personality, and parapsychological experiences (precognition).

PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATORS (PIs):

Helané Wahbeh

FUNDED BY:

The Bial Foundation and the Institute of Noetic Sciences (IONS)

PURPOSE:

Some people sometimes experience a “gut feeling” or premonition of some future event before it happens, without reasoning based on prior knowledge. These experiences are called “precognitive” and one example is dreaming about something that later turns out to occur. This study aims to understand the role of personality and a relaxing environment (ganzfeld) in these experiences.

PROCEDURES

This study entails the screening questionnaires you are completing now, and two 60-minute sessions as the IONS laboratory in Novato, California with study staff.

Screening: First you will complete screening questionnaires that will last about 2-3 minutes and is not compensated, including questions about your prior experience with meditation/artistic activity, parapsychological experiences, whether you think you would succeed in a parapsychological experiment, and time perception. Because of the nature of our research questions, only some participants will be invited to participate in the study sessions. Eligible participants will schedule two study sessions on different days as close to each other as possible as schedules allow.

Session 1: You will arrive at the IONS laboratory in Novato, California with IONS study staff. You will complete several standardized self-report questionnaires. The forms measure your perception of how outgoing and open to experiences you are, how easily you get bored, how immersed or detached you may feel at times (such as when reading a book or watching TV), and whether you believe in parapsychological phenomena. Then you will sit in a reclining chair and be invited to relax for 7 minutes. You will then complete ratings about your experiences and thoughts during these 7 minutes. You will also then play a collaborative game with the study staff. Session 1 will last about 60 minutes.

Session 2: You will arrive at the laboratory and be asked to do a task designed to investigate precognition in a specific environment (ganzfeld). You will first sit in a comfortable reclining chair. You will wear a goggle/headphone combination over your eyes and ears. If you wear glasses you will take them off. The headset will deliver a static-like noise which sounds like a waterfall (i.e., white noise) and the goggles will emit a static red light over your eyes. You will sit in this ganzfeld environment for 35 minutes and describe into a microphone any experiences you have (inner images, thoughts, and feelings). After the 35 minutes, you will complete a questionnaire about your experiences during the ganzfeld. Two randomly selected short film clips will then be shown. You will choose which clip you think represented the experiences you had during the ganzfeld experience.

After submitting your answers, you will be shown which clip was chosen randomly by a computer to be the “target” for your session (even though it was not shown to you before).

COSTS

There is no cost to participate in this study apart from commuting to IONS, which will not be reimbursed. You will receive $20 via Paypal for completing both sessions.

RISKS

Previous studies have used these methods without any reported complications. The ganzfeld is usually reported to be a relaxing experience. If at any point, you feel uncomfortable in the ganzfeld or in reporting your thoughts and experiences, you can stop the experiment by alerting the study staff at any time. You do not need to report anything that is embarrassing or private to you in your experience reports.

BENEFITS

The study can be an opportunity to reflect on your consciousness and various experiences. It can provide a novel experience about how we interpret our world through our senses. Ganzfeld is an interesting, unusual psychological environment as it presents a visual and auditory “homogeneous” field. That is, the white noise and red light are completely the same throughout the 35 minutes. We are used to being constantly flooded with diverse sensory impressions (e.g., in a supermarket, there are lots of sounds and visual objects competing for your attention). In ganzfeld, however, it’s the same red light wherever you look and the same white noise for about 35 minutes, and there is no variation to pay attention to. How we perceive such an environment can be an interesting experience and say something about how our senses and brain work. Although it may also be interesting to participate in a precognitive test, we want to be clear that we are analyzing the results at a group level, so we cannot comment on any individual results. Also, a single study cannot “prove” a phenomenon as there is always uncertainty about an empirical result from a single study.

PARTICIPATION

Participation is entirely voluntary. You do not have to join this or any research study. If you join and later wish to withdraw, we will not keep any data acquired by that point, and you will not receive a prorated payment.

CONFIDENTIALITY

There is always a slight risk of loss of confidentiality. To reduce this risk, all data collected during this study will be anonymized using random unique identifiers (e.g., studyID_0001). Only one Excel sheet will link participants’ information (name, contact information) with their study identifier and will be password-protected (only the Principal Investigator will know the password). Any other documents containing participant’s confidential information will be scanned and stored on the Institute’s Google drive with only the Principal Investigator having access. Hard copies will be shredded. Anonymous data may be shared with the larger research community to advance science and health.

OVERSIGHT

This research is overseen by an Institutional review board (IRB). You may communicate with the IRB Chair, Dr. Garret Yount, at gyount@noetic.org if: 1) your questions, concerns, or complaints are not answered; 2) you want to talk to someone besides the research team; 3) you have questions about your rights as a research subject; 4) you want to get more information or provide input about this research. If you have any concerns or complaints regarding this study now or in the future, or you think you may have been injured or harmed by the study, contact the Director of Research, Dr. Wahbeh, at 707-779-8230.
